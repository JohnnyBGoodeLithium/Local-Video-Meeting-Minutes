## English

### 90-second executive narrative

“This PoC started with a simple problem: dense meetings are expensive to replay, and a generic summary often loses who said what and why we should trust it. The workflow runs locally and turns a Teams recording, audio file, or high-information video into a connected context package.

The transcript becomes usable before every enhancement is finished. I can select a person, listen only to their contributions, and correct a word or identity without losing my place. In the minutes, every important conclusion can return to the original audio and the screen shown at that time.

The same source can then leave the application in three controlled forms: an offline Viewer for a colleague, portable AI Context for a user-selected model, or a document for a local knowledge base. The durable value is identity, evidence, correction, and portability—not dependence on one model. Meeting review is validated in a controlled pilot; video and team RAG remain under evaluation.”

### 5–8 minute product demonstration

1. **Start with one source (30 seconds).** Import a fictional Teams recording with VTT/DOCX, or a synthetic product video. State that processing is local by default.
2. **Show progressive processing (45 seconds).** Point to the current business step, outputs already usable, and ETA range. Explain that the transcript can be reviewed before visual enrichment finishes.
3. **Open the transcript (60 seconds).** Select a fictional speaker, switch between sequential and speaker-only playback, and use previous/replay/next. Do not open administration screens.
4. **Return from minutes to evidence (90 seconds).** Open one conclusion, click its time evidence, play the original words, and show the corresponding visual. State that the visual does not independently prove a decision.
5. **Make one safe correction (60 seconds).** Correct a fictional transcript term, show the revision warning, then use Quick sync. Explain that complete cached visuals are reused and missing cache forces standard regeneration.
6. **Handoff (60 seconds).** Export an offline Viewer or AI Context. If a configured local KB is available, show publication preview and revision behavior; do not demonstrate credentials or private targets.
7. **Close with boundaries (30 seconds).** Controlled PoC, single-machine deployment, no production SSO/ACL, and RAG evaluation still in progress.

### Backup demonstrations — only when asked

- Person correction and mixed-speaker repair.
- Checkpoint recovery after a fictional visual-stage failure.
- Adding a synthetic whiteboard photo as meeting material.
- Product-video argument map and key-frame review.
- Evidence-bound RAG with a fixed synthetic question.

## 中文

### 90 秒管理层口述

“这个 PoC 从一个简单问题开始：信息密集的会议回听成本很高，而普通摘要经常丢失谁说了什么，以及为什么应该相信这条结论。工作流默认在本地运行，把 Teams 录屏、音频文件或高信息密度视频整理成相互关联的上下文包。

逐字稿会在所有增强完成前先变得可用。我可以选择一个人物，只听他的发言，并在不丢失当前位置的情况下修正词语或身份。在会议纪要中，每条重要结论都可以回到原始音频和当时显示的画面。

同一来源可以用三种受控方式离开应用：给同事的离线 Viewer、给用户自选模型的 AI Context，或给本地知识库的文档。长期价值是身份、证据、修正和可移植性，而不是依赖某一个模型。会议回顾已在受控试用中验证；视频和团队 RAG 仍在评估。”

### 5–8 分钟产品演示

1. **从一个来源开始（30 秒）。** 导入一场虚构 Teams 会议及 VTT/DOCX，或一条合成产品视频，说明默认本地处理。
2. **展示渐进处理（45 秒）。** 指出当前业务步骤、已经可用的结果和 ETA 范围，说明视觉增强未完成时逐字稿已经可以核听。
3. **打开逐字稿（60 秒）。** 选择虚构说话人，在顺次播放与仅听此人之间切换，使用上一段、重播、下一段；不打开人员后台。
4. **从纪要回到证据（90 秒）。** 打开一条结论，点击时间依据，播放原话并查看对应画面；强调画面不能独立证明决定。
5. **完成一次安全修正（60 秒）。** 修改一个虚构术语，展示 revision 提示，然后使用“快速同步纪要”。说明完整画面缓存会被复用，缓存缺失时必须走标准重生成。
6. **完成交付（60 秒）。** 导出离线 Viewer 或 AI Context。若本地知识库已配置，只展示发布预检和 revision 行为，不展示凭据或私有目标。
7. **以边界收尾（30 秒）。** 受控 PoC、单机部署、没有生产级 SSO/ACL，RAG 评测仍在进行。
